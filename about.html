About Uganda

Uganda is a beautiful country located in East Africa. It is popularly known as the Pearl of Africa because of its beautiful landscapes, wildlife, forests, lakes, rivers and cultural heritage.

Uganda offers visitors many different tourism experiences. These include wildlife safaris, gorilla trekking, bird watching, mountain hiking, boat cruises and cultural tours.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote Uganda as an attractive tourism destination and provide visitors with useful information about the country's natural beauty, wildlife, culture and tourist attractions.

What Makes Uganda Special?

Wildlife

Uganda has a variety of wildlife including elephants, lions, giraffes, hippos and chimpanzees.

Nature

The country has beautiful forests, lakes, rivers, mountains and waterfalls.

Culture

Uganda has many different communities with unique cultures, traditions, music and dance.

Why Visit Uganda?

  • Beautiful natural scenery
  • Wildlife and national parks
  • Mountain gorilla trekking
  • Rich cultural experiences
  • Bird watching
  • Adventure and outdoor activities
